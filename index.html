A NOTE FROM THE HOUSE

The odds are weird.
That's the point.

Every draw is random within its stated pool. Slot tiers are for entertainment, not real salary or career rankings. No bets, no accounts, and no personal profiles. The catalogue combines a CC BY 4.0 job-title dataset, ESCO occupations, and an extra handpicked collection of unusual and adult-industry titles (non-graphic). Australian descriptions and median weekly earnings are sourced from Jobs and Skills Australia / ABS (May 2025 pay; February 2026 ANZSCO release). The published medians describe full-time non-managerial adult-rate employees within occupation groups, not a guaranteed salary for an individual role. Some roles have only a title-based overview and no reliable pay figure. Job names are not necessarily standardised, available or legally permitted in every region. Nobody can enumerate literally every job in the world.
