Data sources & pay methodology +

Job titles: gpriday/job-titles (CC BY 4.0), ESCO, Jobs and Skills Australia, and curated unusual roles. The catalogue is extensive but not literally every possible job.

Descriptions and salaries: Jobs and Skills Australia (ANZSCO February 2026 release; ABS May 2025 earnings). Pay is an annualised median for matching occupation groups of full-time non-managerial adult-rate employees, not a guaranteed salary. Missing figures are not estimated. Title-based descriptions are labelled in the full profile. Rarity colours are independent of the job; jackpot tiers are entertainment groupings.
